Support

Help for the Duo edition of Lanternbound: Twofold.

Supported play surface

Designed around the iPhone Duo inner display, with the adventure above and controls below. Layouts adapt to the available space, including the outer display. Other compatible iPhone models support landscape play. Requires iOS 27.0 or later. This update is in preparation; this page is not a download offer.

Controls and planning

Use the D-pad to move, A to jump and B for the current instrument. Plan pauses play while you inspect the map and prepare your route; return to live play to continue. Contextual controls vary with the instrument.

Language and sound

Ten languages are included: English, Simplified and Traditional Chinese, French, Spanish, Japanese, German, Italian, Brazilian Portuguese and Korean. Check the game’s sound settings and your device volume if music or effects are silent.

Progress and deletion

Progress and settings are saved locally. Removing the app removes its on-device data; a device backup may retain a copy under your Apple backup settings. We cannot recover a lost local save from a server.

Report a problem

Email wlh96110@gmail.com with your app version, chapter, language and a short description. A screenshot is optional. Please avoid sending passwords, payment information or unrelated personal details.

Support email deletion

To request deletion of correspondence or attachments you previously sent us, email wlh96110@gmail.com from the same address and identify the conversation.
