Lanternbound: Twofold

Privacy

This policy separates the game’s local data from optional support email and this website’s hosting.

2026-10-25

Game data stays on your device

The game requires no account. It does not include advertising, tracking, analytics SDKs or a developer-operated data service. We do not collect gameplay progress or send it to our servers. Save progress, language and sound preferences are stored locally using device storage.

Optional support email

If you email wlh96110@gmail.com, we receive your email address, message and any attachments you choose to send. We use them to respond to your request. Do not include sensitive information that is unnecessary for support. You may request deletion of this correspondence by emailing the same address.

Purchases and Apple services

Apple handles App Store transactions. We do not receive your payment-card details. Apple may process purchase information, diagnostics or device backups under its own settings and privacy policies. Local game data can be included in an Apple-managed device backup.

Website data

This static website adds no analytics, advertising trackers, cookies or contact forms. This website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs visitors’ IP addresses for security. These hosting records are separate from game data and are handled by GitHub under its privacy policy.

Your choices

You can remove the app to remove its on-device data and manage any device backups through Apple’s settings. For support messages or privacy questions, contact wlh96110@gmail.com. The game provides no developer server account or cloud save to delete.

Hosting references

GitHub Pages data collection: https://docs.github.com/en/pages/getting-started-with-github-pages/what-is-github-pages#data-collection · GitHub privacy statement: https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement

Policy updates

This policy is dated 25 October 2026. If the game or hosting practices change, this page will be updated before describing the new practices.
